Questions buyers ask

Straight answers

Is the project RERA registered?

Every Vida project is registered with TS RERA before we sell a single home. The number is on every page and brochure.

What is not included in the price?

Nothing that is not on the price sheet. Stamp duty and registration are paid to the government, and we show those too.

What happens if possession is late?

The agreement carries the date. If we miss it, RERA interest is credited to you every month until handover.

Can I see the site and a sample flat?

Yes. Book a visit and a Vida advisor meets you at the site office.

Do you help with home loans?

We work with leading banks that have pre-approved our projects, so your loan moves faster.
