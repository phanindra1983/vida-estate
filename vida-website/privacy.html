Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Vida Estates, operated by Vibridge Ventures LLP ("we", "us"), handles information when you contact us through our website or message us on WhatsApp.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in an enquiry form, such as your name, phone number, email address and what you are looking for.
  • Messages you send to our WhatsApp business number, along with your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry or message and to follow up about the homes you asked about.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you so our team can help you consistently.

We do not sell your information. We only send you messages about your enquiry, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Who processes it

We use trusted service providers to run these services, including Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), our hosting provider and our workflow and email tools. They process information only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and message records only as long as we need them to help you, or as required by law. After that we delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email us at hello@vidaestates.com or reply "STOP" on WhatsApp.

Data deletion

To have your data deleted, email hello@vidaestates.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the phone number or email you used. We will confirm once it is done.
